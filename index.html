Why Kenya?

Kenya offers many ways to live, from the coast to the cities. What suits you depends on your plans, budget and priorities, so we help you compare options honestly.

Costs, safety and living conditions vary by area and lifestyle. Always do your own research.

  • Coastal livingBeachside towns with a relaxed pace.
  • Diverse housingApartments, houses and gated communities.
  • Tropical climateWarm weather through much of the year.
  • International communitiesEstablished expatriate and diaspora networks.
  • Remote workingConnectivity varies, so check by location.
  • Range of lifestylesDifferent areas suit different budgets.
