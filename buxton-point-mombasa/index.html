Why Buxton Point?

Buxton Point is not just a set of apartments; it is a lifestyle estate. Developed by GulfCap Real Estate, it has been recognised as the Affordable Housing Project of the Year at the Kenya Professional Realtors Association Awards. The development is transforming a 12.5-acre site on Mombasa Island into a modern community.

Amenities that change daily life

When you buy at Buxton Point, you are investing in a community designed for comfort and convenience. Phase 2 residents will enjoy:

  • Olympic-sized swimming pool — perfect for morning laps or weekend relaxation.
  • Fully equipped gymnasium — stay active without leaving the estate.
  • Basketball and football courts — space for sports and active living.
  • Children’s playgrounds and landscaped gardens — safe, green spaces for families.
  • Retail and food court — a minimart and dining options within the estate.
  • Solar-powered lighting and water heating — part of the estate’s sustainable design.

Location and connectivity

Buxton Point sits in Tononoka Ward on Mombasa Island, along Abdel Nasser Road. This central location puts the whole city within easy reach:

  • Fort Jesus — approx. 4 km away.
  • Nyali Cinemax and City Mall — approx. 3 km away.
  • Mombasa CBD, SGR station, and Moi International Airport — easily accessible for commuters and travellers.
  • North Coast beaches — a short drive for weekend escapes.

Residents and guests consistently mention the convenience of the location, the security, and the quality of the amenities.

Completion timeline

Phase 1 is complete and handed over to residents. Phase 2 is currently under construction and is scheduled for completion in August 2027. The new phase will feature 16-storey towers designed to offer sea views from many apartments.
