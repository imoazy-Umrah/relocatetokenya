About Relocate to Kenya

We focus on one thing: helping people move to Kenya and settle in well.

What we do

Relocate to Kenya is based on the coast. We guide people through the practical side of moving here, from choosing where to live to finding a home and getting set up.

We help retirees, expatriates, families, students, professionals and anyone planning to relocate.

We are not a holiday booking site or a travel agency, and we do not give legal advice ourselves.

  • Finding a homeLong-term rentals and properties to buy.
  • Settling inPractical help for your first weeks and months.
  • Starting a businessGuidance on the path to take and who to speak to.
  • Moving and migrationGeneral information and introductions to qualified professionals.

Our local network

Relocating works best with people who know the area. We work with developers and landowners, tour companies and other local service providers, many of them long-standing family friends and contacts.

Legal support

Our legal partner is Bwanaadi Law Firm, a firm connected to our family. Legal work is carried out by the firm, not by Relocate to Kenya, and you are free to use any qualified lawyer you prefer.

Information on this site about visas and permits is general guidance, not legal advice. We cannot promise any visa, permit or outcome.
