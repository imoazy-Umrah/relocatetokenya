About Relocate to Kenya

We focus on one thing: helping people move to Kenya and settle here. We are your go-to solution for visas, housing and everything in between.

What we do

Relocate to Kenya helps people from all over the world make the move, from retirees and expatriates to families, students and investors. We have helped many people, including many Italians, secure their stay through dependant passes, business investment and other routes.

We do not just give advice. We work with you from your first question to your first day in your new home.

  • Visas and permitsDependant passes, business and investment routes and more.
  • Housing anywhere in KenyaLong-term rentals and properties to buy across the country.
  • Settling inDocumentation and all the essentials for your first weeks and months.
  • Starting a businessThe path to take to set up and invest here.

Our local network

Relocating works best with people who know the ground. We work with developers and landowners, tour companies and other local service providers across Kenya, many of them long-standing family friends and contacts.

Legal support

Our legal partner is Bwanaadi Law Firm, a firm connected to our family. It supports our clients with visas, permits, marriage matters and disputes.
